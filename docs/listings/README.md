# Getting Toolbelt listed on the software directories

Three free listings, in the order to do them. Each file in this folder is a
paste-ready kit: open the file, follow the steps, copy the blocks into the
matching fields. Budget ~30 minutes each.

| Order | Site | Why | Kit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Capterra (Gartner Digital Markets) | One form also lists you on GetApp **and** Software Advice — three directories for one effort | `capterra.md` |
| 2 | Trustpilot | Free company profile; where you'll send contractors for reviews | `trustpilot.md` |
| 3 | G2 | The badge people recognize; slowest approval | `g2.md` |

## Ground rules (all three sites)

- **Use the same email everywhere:** support@crewcraftapp.com (set up the
  forwarding first — see the support-email guide in chat). Directories verify
  by emailing the domain, so a @gmail.com address can stall approval.
- **Never seed fake reviews.** Directories delete them and flag the vendor —
  and this niche can smell them anyway. The listings are allowed to sit at
  "no reviews yet"; ask real users after they win a job.
- **Screenshots to prepare once, reuse on all three** (phone-frame or clean
  browser, real-looking data, no personal info):
  1. Estimate builder with AI-drafted line items
  2. The branded estimate the homeowner sees (with the Accept & e-sign button)
  3. An invoice with the PAID stamp
  4. Analytics (win rate / quoted vs won)
  5. The dashboard showing the belt of apps
- **Category to pick when asked:** "Field Service Management" first,
  "Contractor Management" / "HVAC" / "Plumbing" / "Roofing" verticals where
  offered. Avoid generic "Billing & Invoicing" as the primary — you'd compete
  with QuickBooks instead of Housecall Pro.

## The review-ask habit (do this forever)

When a contractor tells you the app won them a job (email reply, support
message, cancellation survey — anywhere), send this within the hour:

> That's awesome to hear — congrats on the job. Quick favor: would you drop
> that story as a 2-sentence review? It's the #1 thing that helps other
> contractors trust a small tool like this. Takes 2 minutes:
> [Capterra link] or [Trustpilot link]. Either one. Thank you!

Fill in the two links once your listings are live. Aim every ask at ONE site
until it has ~10 reviews (start with Capterra), then rotate.
