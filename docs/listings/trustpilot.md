# Trustpilot listing kit

Trustpilot works differently: it's a *company* review page tied to your
domain, and the free plan is all you need. Claiming it takes ~15 minutes.

## Steps

1. Go to **business.trustpilot.com** → **Create free account** (google
   "Trustpilot for business" if the URL moved).
2. Enter the domain **crewcraftapp.com** — this creates/claims the profile
   page at `trustpilot.com/review/crewcraftapp.com`.
3. Sign up with **support@crewcraftapp.com** (same-domain email makes domain
   verification instant; otherwise they ask for a DNS record — if so, add the
   TXT record they show you in the same DNS panel where you set up email
   forwarding).
4. Fill the company profile with the blocks below, upload the Toolbelt logo
   (`public/toolbelt-logo.png`) and the orange icon (`public/icon-512.png`).
5. Skip every paid-plan upsell — automatic review invitations, widgets on
   the paid tier, all of it. The free page + manual asks is the whole play.
6. Copy your public review URL into the review-ask template in `README.md`.

## After it's live

- Put the Trustpilot page link in your email signature.
- Only invite customers who actually used the product (Trustpilot's rules
  and also just correct). One honest 4-star beats three suspicious 5-stars.
- Reply to EVERY review, good or bad, as the founder — replies are public
  and are read more than the reviews themselves.
