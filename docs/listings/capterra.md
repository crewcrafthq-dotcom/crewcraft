# Capterra listing kit (also covers GetApp + Software Advice)

Capterra, GetApp, and Software Advice are all Gartner Digital Markets — one
vendor account lists you on all three.

## Steps

1. Go to **capterra.com** → scroll to the footer → **Vendors: Get listed**
   (or go straight to `digitalmarkets.gartner.com` and click **Get listed /
   Create account**). If the link has moved, google "Capterra get listed".
2. Sign up with **support@crewcraftapp.com**. Company: **Crewcraft**.
3. Submit the product using the blocks below. Approval is a human review —
   expect a few days to a couple of weeks. They may email questions; answers
   are at the bottom of this file.
4. When it's live, grab your listing URL and paste it into the review-ask
   template in `README.md`.

```

materials, priced from your own rate book. Send a branded, e-signable

Built for solo contractors and small crews in HVAC, plumbing, electrical,
and roofing. Mobile-first — it runs from the truck. Free plan (3 estimates,
no credit card), then $29/month flat with everything included. No per-user
fees, no contracts, no setup fees.
```

## Answers they commonly ask for

- **Integrations:** Stripe (payments + subscriptions), Resend (email
  delivery). Estimates/invoices export as PDF.
- **Languages:** App in English; estimate/invoice PDFs in 22 languages
  (English, Spanish, Romanian, and more).
- **Mobile:** Installable web app (Add to Home Screen) — no app-store
  download needed; everything is phone-first.
- **Who is it NOT for:** Large multi-crew operations that need dispatch,
  GPS fleet tracking, or inventory — Toolbelt focuses on quoting, winning,
  and getting paid.
