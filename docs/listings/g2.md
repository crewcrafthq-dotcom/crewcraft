# G2 listing kit

G2 is the slowest of the three (human review, sometimes weeks) and the most
"enterprise" — do it third, but do it: its badges are the ones SaaS buyers
recognize, and the listing is free.

## Steps

1. Go to **g2.com** → footer → **Sell on G2 / Add your product** (or
   `sell.g2.com` → "List your product"; google "list product on G2" if
   moved).
2. Create the account with **support@crewcraftapp.com**. You'll be asked to
   verify you work for the vendor — a same-domain email settles it.
3. Submit the product profile with the blocks below (they're stricter about
   length limits; short versions included).
4. While you wait for approval, don't buy anything — the free profile is
   enough. Decline the "G2 Marketing Solutions" sales call politely.
5. When live, save your product URL for the review-ask rotation (G2 last —
   its review flow is longer, ~10 minutes, so send your keenest fans there).

## Their vetting questions, pre-answered

- **Is this a real, generally available product?** Yes — live at
  crewcraftapp.com with self-serve signup and a free plan; no waitlist.
- **Demo access for the review team:** offer them the landing-page "Try it"
  interactive demo + the free plan (no card needed) — that's usually enough.
- **What makes it different:** flat pricing with everything included, and
  an AI estimate builder where the math is computed (never model-guessed) —
  aimed at owner-operators, not dispatch-heavy fleets.
